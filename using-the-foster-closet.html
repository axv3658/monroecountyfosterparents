Making the most of the Foster Closet

What to include in a clothing request—and how to prepare a useful donation.

Children grow, seasons change, and sometimes a clothing need arrives all at once. The Foster Closet is a way to request essentials or offer clothing another family could use.

If you’re requesting clothes

Start with what would be most useful right now. The request form asks for ages and sizes, the items you need, and how soon you need them.

  • Be specific about sizes, especially for shoes and coats.
  • Put the most-needed items first: for example, a winter coat, pajamas, or school clothes.
  • Include helpful preferences, such as an easy-to-use zipper or a preferred fit.
  • Share your contact information so someone can follow up. You don’t need to include a child’s name or placement history.

The closet will do its best to match your request with donated clothing. Availability depends on what has been donated.

If you’re donating

Use the donation form to describe what you have before arranging a handoff. Include sizes, item types, approximate quantity, and condition. Mention whether you can drop off or need pickup.

Check pockets, pair shoes, and group similar sizes together. A clear description—“one bag of gently used size 6–8 clothes, plus a size 8 coat”—helps someone understand what’s available.

One more step: send the email

Both forms open your email app with the details filled in. Review the message and send it from there; filling in the form alone does not send a request.

If an email app doesn’t open, you can send the same details directly to contact@monroecountyfosterparents.com.
