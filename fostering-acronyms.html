Fostering acronyms: a quick reference

Fostering comes with a lot of new language. Conversations, emails, and paperwork can be full of initials that everyone else seems to know. This list brings together the acronyms we’ve come across, so you have a place to look them up.

Some acronyms have different meanings depending on the agency or setting. These are the meanings we’ve encountered in fostering, with local context where helpful.

The acronym list

504 plan
A school plan for accommodations and services under Section 504 of the Rehabilitation Act.

See NYS guidance on 504 plans.

AFC
Attorney for the Child

In New York Family Court, AFC refers to the child’s attorney. You may have heard “advocate for child.” See New York Courts, Fourth Department.

AP
Adoptive parent
BP
Biological parent (bio parent)
CASA
Court Appointed Special Advocate
CFS
Child and Family Services

The Child and Family Services Division provides child and family welfare services through Monroe County DHS.

CPP
Child-Parent Psychotherapy
CPS
Child Protective Services
CW
Caseworker
DV
Domestic violence
EI
Early Intervention
FD
Foster daughter
FFY
Former foster youth
FP
Foster parent
FS
Foster son
GAL
Guardian ad litem

A person appointed by a judge to protect someone’s interests during a court case. See New York Courts’ guide to guardianship.

ICPC
Interstate Compact on the Placement of Children

An agreement governing placements of children across state lines. See New York State’s ICPC information.

IEP
Individualized Education Program

A written plan describing a student’s special education services and supports. See NYS guidance on IEPs.

IFS
Intensive Family Support

Hillside’s IFS program supports Monroe County foster families and relative placements.

MCDHS
Monroe County Department of Human Services

The county department responsible for foster care, adoption, child protective services, and other assistance. Visit Monroe County Human Services.

OCFS
Office of Children and Family Services

The New York State agency overseeing child welfare services, including foster care and adoption. Visit NYS OCFS.

SPCC
Society for the Prevention of Cruelty to Children

Rochester’s SPCC uses the name Society for the Protection and Care of Children.

SPR
Service Plan Review
SW
Social worker
TFC
Therapeutic foster care
TPR
Termination of parental rights
TVP
Therapeutic Visitation Program
